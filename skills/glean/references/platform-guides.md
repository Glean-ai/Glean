# Platform Guides

Workflow patterns for each outreach platform. The CMO reads this when building platform-specific skills.

## LinkedIn

### Search
- Use LinkedIn search with ICP keywords (title, industry, location)
- Filter by: connections of connections, groups, posts
- URL pattern: `https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/people/?keywords=<query>`

### Extract
- Navigate to each profile
- Extract: name, title, company, location, about section
- Look for: shared connections, recent posts, interests

### Outreach
- **Connection request**: Short, personalized note. Reference something specific from their profile.
- **InMail**: Longer message. Lead with value, not pitch.
- **Follow-up**: Check for accepted connections after 3-5 days. Send thank-you + value message.

### Rules
- Do not send more than 20-30 connection requests per day
- Always personalize — never use generic templates
- Reference something specific from their profile or recent activity
- If they post content, engage with it before connecting

## X/Twitter

### Search
- Search by keywords in bios: `?f=user`
- Search by keywords in posts: `?f=live`
- Follow hashtags relevant to your audience
- Monitor accounts your target audience follows

### Extract
- Visit profiles, extract: name, bio, location, website, recent posts
- Note what they post about — this is outreach material

### Outreach
- **Reply to posts**: Add value to their content. Be helpful, not salesy.
- **Quote tweet**: Share their content with your take. Gets attention.
- **DM**: Only after some interaction. Reference the interaction.
- **Follow + engage**: Follow first, engage with 2-3 posts, then DM.

### Rules
- Never cold DM without prior interaction
- Engage publicly before going private
- Keep DMs short — under 100 words
- Reference specific content they posted

## Email

### Find Emails
- Check LinkedIn profile websites
- Check company websites (About, Team pages)
- Use email pattern: first@domain, first.last@domain
- Verify with mailtester.com or similar

### Outreach
- **Subject line**: Short, specific, relevant to them
- **Body**: 3-5 sentences max. Lead with their problem, not your solution.
- **CTA**: One clear ask. "Would a 15 min call make sense?"
- **Follow-up**: Wait 3-5 days. Add new value, don't just bump.

### Rules
- Personalize every email — reference their company, role, or recent news
- Never mass-blast. Quality over quantity.
- Keep under 150 words
- One CTA only
- Unsubscribe link if sending at scale

## Google Maps

### Search
- Search: `<keyword> in <location>` (e.g., "plumber in Austin")
- Extract from result cards: name, category, rating, review count
- Click into details: address, phone, website, hours, reviews

### Extract
- Visit each business website to find email addresses
- Check for contact forms, about pages, team pages
- Cross-reference with LinkedIn for owner/founder profiles

### Outreach
- **Email**: If email found, send personalized email
- **Phone**: Call if email not available and phone is listed
- **Contact form**: Use as last resort — less personal

### Rules
- Focus on businesses with 4+ stars but under 200 reviews (established but not huge)
- Check if they have a website before outreach
- Reference their reviews or location in outreach
- Local businesses respond better to phone than email
