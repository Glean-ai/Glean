# Marketing Playbooks

Strategy frameworks by business stage. The CMO reads this to decide what to recommend.

## Pre-Revenue (0 users)

Goal: Find your first 10 interested people.

### Strategy
- Pick ONE channel. Do not spread thin.
- Start with the channel where your audience already hangs out.
- Manual outreach only. No automation yet — you need to learn what messaging works.

### Channel Selection
| Audience | Best Channel | Why |
|----------|-------------|-----|
| Developers | X/Twitter, GitHub | They live here |
| Business owners | LinkedIn, Google Maps | Professional context |
| Local businesses | Google Maps | Location-based |
| Consumers | Email, X/Twitter | Broad reach |

### Workflow
1. Search for people matching your target audience
2. Visit their profile — verify they fit your criteria
3. Send personalized outreach (connection request, DM, or email)
4. Track every lead in CSV
5. Follow up once after 3-5 days if no response
6. After 10 leads: review what messaging got responses

### Metrics That Matter
- Response rate (not connection rate)
- Qualification rate (of those who respond, how many are a fit)
- What messaging got replies

## Early Traction (1-10 customers)

Goal: Systematize what worked. Get to 20-50 customers.

### Strategy
- You now know what messaging works. Document it.
- Expand to a second channel.
- Start tracking pipeline metrics seriously.
- Build follow-up sequences.

### Workflow
1. Run the same outreach that worked before, at higher volume
2. Add a second channel (if LinkedIn worked, try Email next)
3. Create follow-up sequences (3-touch over 2 weeks)
4. Track: leads per channel, response rate, conversion rate
5. Kill channels that do not work. Double down on what does.

### Metrics That Matter
- Leads per channel
- Response rate by channel
- Cost per lead (time is cost)
- Conversion rate (lead → customer)

## Scaling (10+ customers)

Goal: Grow beyond manual outreach. Build repeatable systems.

### Strategy
- Automate the repetitive parts (but keep personalization)
- Consider hiring or outsourcing prospecting
- Build content that attracts inbound leads
- Test paid channels if unit economics work

### Workflow
1. Optimize your best-perverting channel first
2. Add automation for search and extraction (keep messaging personal)
3. Build content that drives inbound (blog, social, community)
4. Test one paid channel with small budget
5. Hire a VA for prospecting if time is the bottleneck

### Metrics That Matter
- Monthly lead volume
- Pipeline velocity (leads → customers per month)
- Customer acquisition cost (CAC)
- Lifetime value (LTV) vs CAC ratio
