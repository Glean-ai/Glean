# Community Rules

How to engage in communities without being spammy.

## The 80/20 Rule

- 80% of your activity should be genuinely helpful
- 20% can mention your product (only when relevant)

## Before You Post

1. Read the community rules (sidebar, wiki)
2. Lurk for a few days to understand the culture
3. See what kind of posts get upvoted
4. See what gets downvoted or banned

## What to Post

### Good
- Answer questions with genuine expertise
- Share useful resources (not just your product)
- Tell stories about your experience
- Ask thoughtful questions
- Celebrate others' wins

### Bad
- "Check out my product!"
- "We just launched X, here's the link"
- "I built this, would love feedback" (in wrong communities)
- Posting the same thing in multiple communities
- Creating accounts just to promote

## When to Mention Your Product

Only when:
- Someone explicitly asks for recommendations
- You've established yourself as a helpful member
- The product is genuinely relevant to the discussion
- You disclose your relationship ("I'm the founder, but...")

## Building Reputation

1. Be helpful first, always
2. Share knowledge freely
3. Be honest about limitations
4. Engage consistently over weeks/months
5. Let people discover your product through your helpfulness

## Tracking

When you get a lead from a community:
- Add to leads.csv with source=community
- Note which community
- Track what discussion led to the lead
