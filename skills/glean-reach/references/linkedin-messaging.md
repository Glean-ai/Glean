# LinkedIn Messaging Workflow

How to send connection requests and messages on LinkedIn using BrowserAct.

## Connection Request

### Compose

Short, personal note. Under 300 characters. No pitch.

Structure:
1. Reference something specific from their profile
2. Express genuine interest
3. No ask, no pitch

Examples:
- "Hi [Name], saw your post about [topic] — really resonated with me. Would love to connect."
- "Hey [Name], noticed you're [doing X at Company]. I've been thinking about that problem too. Let's connect."
- "Hi [Name], we're both in the [industry] space. I'd love to follow your work. Let's connect."

### Preview

Show the founder:
"Connection request to John Smith (CTO @ Acme):
'Hi John, saw your post about scaling engineering teams — really resonated. Would love to connect.'
Send this?"

### Send

After approval, navigate to profile and send connection request.

## InMail (if available)

For 2nd/3rd degree connections without connection request:

Structure:
1. Reference connection or mutual interest
2. Lead with their problem, not your solution
3. One clear CTA

Example:
"Hi [Name], I noticed you're scaling [Company] — congrats on the growth. We're both working on [problem]. I'd love to hear how you're approaching it. Would a 15 min call make sense?"

## Follow-Up Sequence

### Day 3-5: Thank You (if connected)
"Thanks for connecting, [Name]. I've been following your work on [topic]. curious — how are you handling [problem]?"

### Day 7-10: Value Add
"Hey [Name], I came across this [resource/article/data] about [topic]. Thought you might find it interesting: [link]"

### Day 14: Final Follow-Up
"Hey [Name], I know you're busy. Just wanted to reach out one more time about [problem]. If you're open to chatting, I'd love to hear your perspective. If not, no worries — I'll keep following your work."

## Rules

- Never send more than 20-30 connection requests per day
- Always personalize — never use generic templates
- Reference something specific from their profile
- If they post content, engage with it before connecting
- Stop if rate limited
- Track every message in leads.csv
