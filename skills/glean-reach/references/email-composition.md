# Email Composition Workflow

How to write and send cold emails using BrowserAct.

## Subject Line

Short, specific, relevant to them. Under 50 characters.

Formulas:
- "Question about [their company]"
- "[Mutual connection] suggested I reach out"
- "Quick thought on [their recent post/news]"
- "[Specific observation about their company]"

Examples:
- "Question about Acme's onboarding"
- "Saw your post about scaling"
- "Quick thought on your pricing page"

## Body

3-5 sentences max. Under 150 words. Lead with their problem, not your solution.

Structure:
1. Why you're writing (specific to them)
2. The problem you both care about
3. One sentence about your approach
4. Clear CTA

Example:
"Hi [Name],

I noticed [Company] is [specific observation — hiring, growing, launching something]. 

I've been thinking about [problem they likely have] — we're solving it at [Your Company] by [one sentence approach].

Would a 15 min call make sense to compare notes?"

## CTA

One clear ask. Don't overload.

Good CTAs:
- "Would a 15 min call make sense?"
- "Are you open to a quick chat?"
- "Would you be interested in seeing how we approach this?"

Bad CTAs:
- "Let me know if you want to learn more, see a demo, check out our pricing, or schedule a call"
- "Can we hop on a call sometime this week?"

## Personalization

Reference something specific:
- Their company's recent news or growth
- A post they wrote
- A mutual connection
- Something from their LinkedIn profile
- Their company's website or product

## Follow-Up Sequence

### Day 3-5: First Follow-Up
"Hi [Name], just bumping this up. I noticed [new observation]. Would love to hear your thoughts."

### Day 7-10: Value Add
"Hi [Name], I came across this [resource] about [topic]. Thought you might find it relevant: [link]. Still happy to chat if you're interested."

### Day 14: Final Follow-Up
"Hi [Name], I know you're busy. Last note from me — if you ever want to chat about [problem], I'm here. Otherwise, I'll keep following [Company]'s progress."

## Sending

1. Compose email
2. Preview for founder: "Sending this email to Jane Doe (jane@techco.com): [show email]"
3. Wait for approval
4. Send via Gmail or email tool
5. Update leads.csv

## Rules

- Personalize every email — reference their company, role, or recent news
- Never mass-blast. Quality over quantity.
- Keep under 150 words
- One CTA only
- Stop if bounce rate is high
