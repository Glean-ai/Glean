# X/Twitter DM Workflow

How to send DMs on X/Twitter using BrowserAct.

## Pre-DM Engagement

Before sending a DM, engage with their content:

1. **Follow them** (if not already following)
2. **Like 2-3 of their recent posts** (genuine likes, not spam)
3. **Reply to one post** with a helpful, genuine comment
4. **Wait 1-2 days** for the engagement to register
5. **Then send DM**

## DM Structure

Short. Under 100 words. Reference the interaction.

Structure:
1. Reference the interaction (their post, your reply)
2. One sentence about shared interest
3. Soft ask

Example:
"Hey [Name], enjoyed your post about [topic]. I've been thinking about the same problem — we're approaching it differently at [Your Company]. Would love to hear your take. Open to chatting?"

## Follow-Up Sequence

### Day 3-5: Value Add
"Hey [Name], came across this about [topic] — thought you'd find it interesting: [link]"

### Day 7-10: Final
"Hey [Name], last note from me. If you ever want to chat about [problem], I'm here. Otherwise, keep building!"

## Rules

- NEVER cold DM without prior interaction
- Engage publicly before going private
- Keep DMs short — under 100 words
- Reference specific content they posted
- If they don't respond after 2 follow-ups, stop
- Track in leads.csv
